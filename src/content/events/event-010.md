Wait is finally over. Here we present:
**📢 Pi Day Challenge: The Lost Cipher of Archimedes!**

Celebrate Pi Day with a thrilling mathematical treasure hunt! 🏆 Hidden within the digits of π is a secret message—will you be the one to decode it? 

🔍 Your Task:\
Follow the clues, uncover the hidden numbers, and break the cipher to uncover the secret message! The answer is waiting to be revealed. 

📅 Date: March 14 to March 21

🎯 Who Can Join? Everyone—including faculty, students, and puzzle enthusiasts! 

Rules & Regulations :
1. Follow the riddle’s steps to uncover the hidden message. 
2. The first three correct answers will win: 
 - 1st & 2nd place: Perplexity T-shirt + 1-year DataCamp subscription 
 - 3rd place: Mug + 1-year DataCamp subscription 
 - Faculty winner: 1-year DataCamp subscription + the ability to choose 5 students to win a 1-year DataCamp subscription 
3. T-shirts and mugs will be provided only to winners from Kathmandu University Central Campus, Dhulikhel. 
4. Share your answer on your story and this post's comment section mentioning KUMSC's page. 
5. 5️⃣Mention 2 of your friends living outside Kathmandu Valley in this post's comment. 
6. Only individual entries are allowed—no team submissions. 
7. 7️⃣The winners will be announced on our social media on March 23.

🏆 Prizes:
- 1st & 2nd place: Perplexity T-shirt + 1-year DataCamp subscription or gift DataCamp subscription to your friend
- 3rd place: Mug + 1-year DataCamp subscription or gift DataCamp subscription to your friend
- Faculty winner: 1-year DataCamp subscription + the ability to give 5 students of their choice a 1-year DataCamp subscription 

Best of luck, and may the numbers guide you! 🔢✨
